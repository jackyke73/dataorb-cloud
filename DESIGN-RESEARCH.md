# Dataorb Cloud — cloud and data-center design research

Reviewed September 10, 2026. These are visual observations of leading official sites, not a market-share ranking. Regional and campaign variants change; AWS and Google Cloud rendered Chinese-localized pages in this browser.

| Official reference | Theme observations | Applied to Dataorb Cloud |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/) | White primary navigation, dark utility bar, dimensional colorful hero geometry, a clear isolated text panel and strong CTA. | Compact white brand/navigation strip and strong separation between navigation, hero and product content. Simplified the much larger product navigation. |
| [Microsoft Azure](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us) | White navigation, blue primary buttons, expansive split hero, dimensional blue/teal forms, small uppercase eyebrow above a larger sans-serif headline. | Blue actions, cyan accents, generous split composition and an original layered architecture diagram. |
| [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/) | White space, centered large headline, simple filled/outlined actions; dark abstract technical visuals further down. | Short hierarchy, readable typography and limited actions; contrast between technical dark surfaces and readable light sections. |
| [Equinix](https://www.equinix.com/) | Compact white logo/navigation bar; deep-blue cinematic hero with planetary horizon, large type and paired CTAs. | Midnight-blue opening with restrained spatial depth. No globe, facility footprint or implied global capacity. |
| [Digital Realty](https://www.digitalrealty.com/) | Monochrome logo on light navigation, very large light-weight white type over dark infrastructure imagery, prominent contact action. | Oversized but concise heading and direct quote route. No borrowed facility photography or claims of ownership. |
| [CoreWeave](https://www.coreweave.com/) | Black wordmark on white, cobalt accent, left-aligned bold text paired with large dimensional blue forms, rounded contact button. | Approved logo on white, cobalt action color and disciplined split layout. Original geometry rather than copied branded forms. |

## Implemented direction (superseded September 10, 2026)

Midnight (#070f1c) and navy (#0b1524) form the technical opening. Ice-blue headlines and restrained cyan details distinguish hierarchy without animated neon. The hero is an original editable SVG architecture diagram showing compute layers and interconnection; it is conceptual, not a live system or picture of owned hardware. The approved logo remains the original PNG, displayed in the navigation and footer on white.

GPU options use individual bordered navy panels with consistent memory/configuration hierarchy. Expertise, process and quote sections use off-white and cool-gray surfaces for longer reading and form usability. Self-hosted Inter pairs with system monospace for small technical labels. The illustration remains visible and scales down on phones. No added animation or external runtime dependencies.

All existing inquiry safeguards remain: reference specifications rather than inventory, supplier-confirmed terms, and explicitly local-only request drafts until a backend and privacy policy are configured.

## Enterprise infrastructure direction — September 10, 2026

The previous direction tested as "AI-generated" rather than as infrastructure: a two-tone gradient headline, a decorative
isometric diagram that depicted no real system, numbered monospace eyebrows, `↗` glyphs on every link, and marketing cards
where operators publish specifications. A second review looked at how companies that actually run compute present themselves.

| Official reference | Theme observations | Applied to Dataorb Cloud |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CoreWeave](https://www.coreweave.com/) | White hero, small type, utility navigation with search and login, content cards immediately below the fold. | Compact sticky header, small type scale and a utility bar above the navigation. |
| [Crusoe](https://crusoe.ai/) | Hairline rules dividing the page into a visible grid, hard-edged controls, no gradients or glow. | 2 px radii, hairline section rules and flat surfaces throughout. |
| [Vantage](https://vantage-dc.com/) | Utility bar, mega navigation, and a hard band of figures directly under the hero. | A band under the hero in the same position, carrying scope statements instead of figures, because Dataorb Cloud has no operating statistics to publish. |
| [STACK Infrastructure](https://www.stackinfra.com/) | Full-bleed facility photography carrying the entire hero. | Not applied. Dataorb Cloud owns no facilities, and borrowed or generated photography would imply ownership. The hero uses a specification panel instead. |

### Implemented direction

White and cool gray surfaces with a single deep blue accent (`#0f4c96`); the dark utility bar, capability band and footer are the
only dark surfaces. Body type is 15 px, headings top out near 44 px, and figures use tabular numerals. Reference GPU data moved
from four marketing cards into one specification table with real column headers, and the process section became a four-column
table stating what the client provides, what we do and what they receive at each stage. A scope-and-limitations block states
plainly that Dataorb Cloud does not own data centers, hold inventory or guarantee availability.

Removed as visual tells rather than information: the gradient headline, the isometric SVG, the `01 /` monospace eyebrows,
`FIG. 001`, and every decorative arrow glyph. Monospace is now used only for the generated request draft.

The approved logo PNG is unchanged and is never recolored; on the dark footer it sits on a white plate. All existing inquiry
safeguards remain: reference specifications rather than inventory, supplier-confirmed terms, and explicitly local-only request
drafts until a backend and privacy policy are configured.
